Task: E-Commerce Checkout Flow

Take Product Price
Take Quantity
Take Available Stock
Take User Balance

IF Quantity <= 0
    Show "Invalid Quantity"

ELSE IF Quantity > Available Stock
    Show "Not Enough Stock"

ELSE
    Total Price = Product Price * Quantity

    IF Total Price > User Balance
        Show "Insufficient Balance"

    ELSE
        Updated Balance = User Balance - Total Price
        Updated Stock = Available Stock - Quantity

        Show "Checkout Successful"
        Show "Remaining Balance:", Updated Balance
        Show "Remaining Stock:", Updated Stock
